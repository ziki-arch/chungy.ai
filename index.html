Shepherd's Verse

Privacy Policy

Effective October 1, 2026

Shepherd's Verse does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your game stays on your device.

Who we are

Shepherd's Verse (Android package ai.chungy.verse) is a Bible-verse collecting game made by an independent developer ("we", "us"). This policy covers the Shepherd's Verse app only.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect your name, email address, age, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, advertising ID or any other personal or sensitive information. The app has no sign-up, no account, no chat and no way to upload anything.

We do not use advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking services of any kind.

What stays on your device

Your game progress and settings (verses collected, game rounds won, sound and text preferences) are saved in a file inside the app's private storage on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

Android cloud backup is turned off for this app, so the file is not copied to your Google account. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Optional gifts and Google Play

The game is free. A grown-up may choose to leave an optional thank-you gift, which unlocks a cosmetic Supporter badge and nothing else. The gift screen sits behind a grown-ups check.

Gifts are bought and paid for through Google Play's billing system. Google processes the payment under the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). We never receive your name, email address or payment details.

So the badge can be shown or restored, the app asks Google Play whether this device's Google account owns a gift. That check, and Google's billing library that performs it, are the only network activity in the app, and they talk to Google Play only. The billing library may send Google technical information it needs to process purchases and keep the store working.

Permissions

Vibration: for gentle taps and buzzes during play. You can turn it off under Settings.

Internet, network state and billing: added by Google's billing library and used only for gifts as described above. The game itself plays fully offline.

The app does not ask for location, camera, microphone, contacts, phone, or access to your files.

Sharing

We do not share, sell or rent any data, because we do not have any. Apart from Google Play processing a gift you choose to buy, no third party receives information from the app.

Children

Shepherd's Verse is made for players of all ages, including children. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we collect none from children under 13 (or the age set by the law where you live). Purchases need a grown-up's approval through the grown-ups check and Google Play's own purchase controls.

Your choices

You can delete everything the app keeps by uninstalling it, or by clearing the app's storage in your device settings. Purchase history is held by Google; you can review it in your Google Play account.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at the same address and in the app, with a new effective date.

Contact
